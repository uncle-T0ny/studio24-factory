# Studio24 Factory

## Why I built this

My wife is a wedding planner. I love prototyping, and as an experienced developer I know how to get to a stable result: it's a pleasure to see a prototype become not just something that might break, but a production-ready solution. The day before this take-home, I spent 2 hours 40 minutes building an app that covers what she needs, and it saves her a $30-a-month subscription. Back in Vancouver, before Kitsi, I'd worked on a similar idea. So I thought: why not a factory that builds apps like this quickly, efficiently and well? The quality comes from good reference templates: the better the template, the better the app that comes out.

I also went back through the prompts from those 2 hours 40 minutes and saw how much of it could be automated and improved. Studio24 Factory is the result: an example of full-cycle development, from a client's problem to a working solution in production that does its job.
