**Agent:** Hello, and thanks for reaching out! To start, could you tell me what the app should do, and who will be using it?

**Client:** I'm an event planner, and I need a very easy app to make a floor plan of a venue for my clients, indoors or outdoors, with or without the venue's exact measurements. I place tables, get the chairs from the guest count, add my own items, and lay out the ceremony rows. The couple should be able to edit the plan through a link, without signing up. At the end I export a PDF to send to the venue.

**Agent:** Thanks, that's clear. Next: when a couple edits through the link, should their changes go straight to your plan, or do you want to approve each change before it's saved?

**Client:** go straight to your plan

**Agent:** Got it, changes save straight to your plan. Next: roughly how many floor plans would you create per month, and how many couples or guests would open a shared link for each?

**Client:** 4-8 plans a month; 50-300 guests; the link only for couples

**Agent:** Got it: 4-8 plans a month, with links used only by the couples. Which devices will you and the couples mainly use to build and edit the plans: desktop, tablet, or phone?

**Client:** desktop

**Agent:** Got it, desktop for you. Will the couples open the shared link mainly on phones, tablets, or desktops, and does your 50-300 guest count mean the chairs must update live as guests change?

**Client:** right

**Agent:** Just to confirm: you said 'right' to the couples' device question, but I need the specific device. Will couples mainly open the shared link on phones, tablets, or desktops? And should chairs update live when the guest count changes?

**Client:** they'll use desktops, if they try to open from mobile, they will be asked to use desktop and And should chairs update live when the guest count changes? - yes

**Agent:** Thanks, that's clear: couples use desktops, and chairs update live with the guest count. Next, what is your deadline for the first working version, and what is your rough budget or any tools you must connect to, such as a PDF or calendar tool?

**Client:** I have no deadline

**Agent:** Thanks. Since there's no deadline, what budget range are you working within, and must the app connect to any tools you already use, such as a calendar, email, or a venue's own system?

**Client:** no, we just need to be able to upload ready to use floor plans to the app

**Agent:** Got it: you'll upload ready-made floor plans to the app. What budget range are you working within, and should the couples' links expire after the event date?

**Client:** after the event date? - yes; budget 1000$

**Agent:** Thanks. To confirm: the budget is $1,000, and couples' links should expire after the event date. What file formats should the uploaded floor plans come in, such as PDF, image, or CAD files?

**Client:** PDF

**Agent:** Thanks, that's clear: uploaded floor plans come as PDFs. Before you test this idea, what is the riskiest assumption? For example, would couples really edit seating layouts through a link without signing up, and would they trust it?

**Client:** Yes, thanks
